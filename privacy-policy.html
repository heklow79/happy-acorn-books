Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Happy Acorn Books respects the privacy of readers, parents, and families. This policy explains information practices for our informational website at happyacornbooks.com. It does not cover the separate Tiny Animal Town companion app, which is still in development.

Information you choose to send us

Our website currently does not have account registration, checkout, or an online contact form. If you choose to email us at hello@happyacornbooks.com, we receive the details you send, which may include your email address, name, and message content. We use that information to read and respond to your inquiry and handle related communications.

Website hosting and technical information

Our website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes when GitHub Pages sites are visited. For more information about GitHub's practices, please see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Third-party services and links

Our website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Requests for those font files are handled by Google under its applicable policies. Our contact email uses an email-forwarding service, and messages may be processed by the services involved in delivering them.

We may link to third-party sites, including booksellers, app stores, and social media pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices. We encourage adults to review those policies before sharing information with a third party.

Cookies and analytics

At the time of this policy, the website code does not include an advertising pixel, a visitor analytics tag, or a website account system. Third-party services used to deliver the website and fonts may process technical request data. If we introduce tracking, advertising, or additional data-collection features, we will update this policy as appropriate.

Children's privacy

Our books and activities are made for families with young children. This website is intended to provide product information to parents, caregivers, and educators. We do not invite children to send us personal information. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please email hello@happyacornbooks.com so we can review your request and take appropriate action.

Retention and requests

We retain correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond to inquiries and maintain related records, subject to applicable requirements. You may contact us to ask about a message you sent or request its deletion, and we will review your request in light of any applicable obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website features or information practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.

Contact us

For questions about this policy, please contact hello@happyacornbooks.com.
